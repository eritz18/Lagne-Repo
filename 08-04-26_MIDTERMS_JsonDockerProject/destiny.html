How This Chamber Works

Every reading pulled from the Oracle draws from the same twelve-month archive that powers this entire site: a single JSON record per month, holding a creature, a power, a symbol, an artifact, a spirit guide, an alignment, a set of traits, sacred numbers, and a fragment of lore. Choosing a month on the wheel, typing it into the gate, or arriving here from another chamber's gate all resolve to the very same record.

You may also choose an optional Fortune Category — Love, Career, Wealth, Health, Family, Adventure, Destiny, or Spiritual Growth — drawn from its own archive. A category never replaces your base reading; it simply layers a second fragment on top of it, read through whichever part of life is on your mind.
